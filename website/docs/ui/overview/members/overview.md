The Monitoring Service supports multi-tenant user management, allowing the tenant\_admin to securely invite and manage additional users to collaborate within their specific tenant workspace.

![](/img/ui/image22.png)

To manage access, Tenant administrators navigate to the members management page, which is organized into the following functional tabs:

* **Members**:   
  * Displays the full list of all active users currently belonging to the tenant.  
  * Allows (super, tenant) administrators to audit who has access and review their assigned operational roles.  
* **Add Member:**   
  * A direct administrative action reserved primarily for **Super Administrators**.  
  * Super Administrators can immediately provision tenant access by entering a user's email address directly, bypassing the standard invitation acceptance workflow.  
* **Invite Members**:   
  * The standard workflow used by Tenant Administrators to send formal access requests to external or internal users.  
  * *Note: The specific operational steps for this process are detailed in the **Members/Invitations \- Creating an Invitation** section.*  
* **Invitations**:   
  * Displays a central log tracking the life cycle of all sent invitations (Pending, Accepted, or Rejected).  
  * **Key Benefit:** Invitations provide a secure, decentralized, and controlled mechanism to grant tenant access. This removes the need for manual, case-by-case intervention from the core Monitoring Team while ensuring users receive the precise level of privilege required for their responsibilities.  
  * *Note: The specific operational steps for this process are detailed in **Members/Invitations.***
