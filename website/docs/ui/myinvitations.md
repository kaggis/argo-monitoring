## Overview 
The Monitoring Service supports multi-tenant user management, allowing **Tenant Administrators** to securely invite and manage additional users to collaborate within their specific tenant workspace. This is done by inviting members to different tenants and with different roles.

## Invitations 

To manage invitations the user should login to the service and visit My Invitations.

![](/img/ui/image43.png)

![](/img/ui/image35.png)

Upon receiving an invitation you may accept or reject by clicking the correct icon.
